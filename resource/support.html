Description

Ung Shop makes online shopping simple and convenient. Browse available products, view details and prices, add items to your cart, and place orders directly from the app.

You can manage item quantities, review your total before ordering, and track your order status from pending to completed or canceled. The app also supports customer profiles and delivery location setup, so eligible customers can choose free delivery within supported service areas or pick up items at the shop.
